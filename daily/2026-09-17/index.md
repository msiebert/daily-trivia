    question: "Vincent van Gogh painted The Starry Night in June 1889 while a patient at what psychiatric institution near Saint-Remy-de-Provence, France?"
    answer: "Saint-Paul-de-Mausole"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Van Gogh voluntarily admitted himself to Saint-Paul-de-Mausole, a
      former monastery converted into a psychiatric hospital, in May
      1889 after a severe mental health crisis in Arles that included
      the incident where he cut off part of his own ear. He stayed for
      about a year, and it was there in June 1889 that he painted The
      Starry Night, working mostly from memory and imagination rather
      than his usual practice of painting directly from observation. His
      barred, east-facing bedroom window looked out over rolling hills
      and a village, a view that shaped the painting's composition even
      though he embellished and rearranged much of what he actually saw.
      The site still stands today, partly functioning as a psychiatric
      facility and partly as a museum dedicated to Van Gogh's time there.
  - id: "2026-09-17-2"
    question: "Which New York museum has held Van Gogh's The Starry Night in its permanent collection since 1941?"
    answer: "MoMA"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      The Museum of Modern Art in New York acquired The Starry Night in
      1941, and it has remained one of the centerpieces of the museum's
      painting collection ever since. It's consistently one of the most
      visited and photographed works in the building, drawing crowds
      that can make it hard to get a close, quiet look at the actual
      brushwork. The painting rarely travels for loans given its
      fragility and popularity, so seeing it in person almost always
      means a trip to MoMA itself. Its presence there has helped make it
      one of the most reproduced images in Western art, appearing on
      everything from posters to phone cases.
  - id: "2026-09-17-3"
    question: "Vincent van Gogh wrote to which brother, an art dealer in Paris, dismissing his own painting The Starry Night as an inferior work made largely from imagination?"
    answer: "Theo"
    topic: arts
    explanation: >-
      Theo van Gogh worked as an art dealer in Paris and supported his
      brother Vincent both financially and emotionally for most of his
      career, and the two exchanged hundreds of letters that survive
      today. In one of them, Vincent described The Starry Night as a
      failure, since he generally distrusted paintings made from memory
      and imagination rather than direct observation of a real scene.
      Despite Vincent's own low opinion of it, the painting went on to
      become one of the most celebrated and recognizable works in
      Western art history. Theo's careful preservation of Vincent's
      letters is a major reason historians know so much about how and
      why the painting was made.
